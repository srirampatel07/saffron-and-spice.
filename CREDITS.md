# Photo credits

Saffron & Spice uses local, licensed food photography. The site does not hotlink images or depend on a stock-photo CDN. Every photograph below was downloaded, cropped, resized to its display use and converted to WebP for this demo.

- **32 photographs:** 29 dish photos and 3 editorial scenes.
- **3 dishes still use generated SVG placeholders:** Laal Maas, Peshawari Naan and Gulab Jamun Cheesecake. No accurate, appropriately licensed and appetising match was found for these dishes.
- The original 35 SVG illustrations remain in `client/public/images/` as graceful error fallbacks. The site loads a photo first and switches to the matching SVG if the photo fails.

## Processing notes

- Dish images are 800 × 600 (4:3), WebP, quality 78 and under 150 KB each.
- Hero image is 1920 × 2400 (4:5), WebP, quality 28 and under 350 KB.
- Story image is 900 × 1100 and the visit image is 1200 × 900.
- Samosa Chaat and Sarson Salmon Tikka crops remove distracting hands at the edges. Papad & Pickle is cropped toward the papad and pickle. Mango Lassi is cropped around the glass.
- Dal Makhani, Awadhi Lamb Biryani and Sarson Salmon Tikka received slight brightness/saturation adjustments only.
- Licences: Pexels photos use the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Wikimedia Commons photos use CC0 1.0, CC BY 2.0 or CC BY-SA 4.0 as listed.
